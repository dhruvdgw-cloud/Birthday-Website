    Before You Join the Flock

    What does the full day look like?
    • 11:00 AM: Darshan, bhajans, blessings, and lunch
    • 3:00 PM to 6:00 PM: Flamingo Pool Party at Country Inn
    • 6:00 PM onwards: Time to relax, refresh, and dress up
    • 9:00 PM onwards: Dinner, music, and celebration at Morgans
    What should I bring for the pool party?
    • Swimwear
    • Colorful sunglasses
    • Sunscreen
    • A fresh change of clothes
    • Your best poolside energy
    What's the only rule for the day?
    Leave your worries at the door. Come ready to laugh freely, eat happily, make memories, and simply enjoy the day together.
    Should I bring a gift?
    Your presence is the real gift. Your blessings, laughter, and willingness to dance would be a lovely bonus.
    What's the only thing I shouldn't forget?
    Please don't forget to come. The day will be special because of the people in it—and I would truly love to celebrate it with you.
