    Chapter IThe Beginning

    A small girl with enormous eyes.

    Before the awards, the boardrooms, the plane tickets — there was a child somewhere in a room full of laughter, being told she could become anything. And believing it.

    • The house that smelled of ghee and warm cotton.
    • A mother's hand at the back of my head.
    • The first time someone said, "you are meant for something."

    "Every long journey begins with someone loving you first."

    "I used to write my dreams on the last page of my notebook. Then I ran out of pages."
    — a teenager, still learning
    Chapter IIGrowing Up

    Uniforms, crushes, and a very big plan.

    Somewhere between chalkboards and canteens, the plan began to form — scribbled between physics equations and forwarded jokes on Nokia phones.

    Biggest dream, aged 17

    To build something with my name on it, and enough space inside for everyone I love.

      Chapter IVOreo

      A tiny heartbeat that runs this house.

      He does not care about companies, countries, or crores. He cares about 6 PM, and whether I am home yet. Which — turns out — is the only measure that matters.

      "You taught me that love is small, and warm, and shows up every single day — even when it doesn't understand a single word you've said."

      — for my Oreo

      Chapter VLife's Toughest Chapters

      Rain doesn't ruin the story. It writes it.

      A divorce that rewrote what love should mean. Health scares that reminded me the body is a home, not a machine. Family that grew in shape. And the slow, unglamorous miracle of putting yourself back together — one Tuesday at a time.

      Softer

      with people, especially the ones going through their own storm.

      Braver

      about asking for help, and giving it without keeping score.

      Slower

      to judge — everyone is fighting on a battlefield you cannot see.

      "Every chapter made me who I am today.
      I would not trade one line of it."

      Chapter VIDreams Ahead

      The next forty.

      A skyline in Singapore. Brands that mean something on more than one continent. Rooms full of people I helped grow. And enough breathing room in the calendar to actually enjoy any of it.
